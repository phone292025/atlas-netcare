# Decision Log

> **Last updated:** 2026-08-29

Small calls that affect other people but do not warrant a full ADR. One line each. Newest at the top.

**Write an entry when:** you rename something shared, change a shared file, choose between two equally reasonable implementations, or discover something that changes how someone else should work.

**Write an [ADR](../decisions/) instead when:** the decision shapes the architecture, adds a dependency, or someone will later ask "why is it like this?"

| 2026-08-30 | **Missing evidence is not zero risk: an unmeasurable factor is dropped and the remaining weights renormalised.** The naive alternative, counting a null as 0, makes a machine with no purchase date score 25% lower than an identical machine that has one — and sink in the maintenance ranking for the sole reason that nobody typed a date in. `RiskScoringService::combine()` divides by the weight that was actually available, and **rescales the stored breakdown to match**, so the factors on screen still sum to the total above them.
