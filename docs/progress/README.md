## The weekly report is marked

Guidelines Section I: submit a report **every week from week 1 until at least week 12**. The rubric allocates 10 marks to it, and 10 more to "managing work independently and meeting deadlines".

Backdating a stack of reports in week 12 is transparent and scores badly. Fill each week in during that week.

### Required contents

1. **Section 1** — a one-detail description of progress **for each member**. Every member writes their own; the group leader assembles.
2. **Section 2** — any issues relating to project work. Issues uncovered must be addressed immediately.
3. **Section 3** — minutes of the group discussion: positive decisions made, problems solved.
4. **Section 4** — scheduled workload of all members (Gantt or similar chart).

### Rules

- **All submission goes through the group leader.**
- Every member needs their own section. A report with four names in it, when there are seven members, is visibly incomplete.
- Missed deadlines need an Extenuating Circumstances appeal.

## Naming

`week-01.md` through `week-14.md`. Copy the template in `week-01.md` for each new week.
